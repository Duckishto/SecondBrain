# 02-Research

## 🎯 Goal
To conduct structured literature reviews, explore cutting-edge technologies or scientific topics, and document experimental findings and hypotheses.

## 📁 Subfolder Structure
*(Recommended structure for this section)*
- `papers/`: Summaries and notes on academic papers, articles, and whitepapers.
- `experiments/`: Research logs, hypothesis testing, data analysis, and results.
- `ideas/`: Brainstorming sessions, future research directions, and exploratory questions.
